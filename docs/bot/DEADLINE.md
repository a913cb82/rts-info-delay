# Deadline-timing (peak at t10000)

## Physics
- Score is a SNAPSHOT at t10000 (highest wins; no averaging).
- Colony growth: found -> grow (~2000t to mature) -> plateau/decay.
  Early foundings (t500) plateau by t4000 and decay to deadline.
- Timed wave: foundings at t7500-8500 mature exactly at t10000 (peak!).
- v3 founds early (convenience, not timing). Late-wave should outscore
  early-sprawl on snapshot math: same mouths, younger curve.
- Falsifiable in league: wave (foundings only t7500+) vs v3.
- Risk: late pioneers die in traffic (funnel-starvation); 2500t may not
  mature 30-colonies (marginal); capital carries anyway (726-floor).
