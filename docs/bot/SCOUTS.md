# Scout-fan (effective small-army scouting)

## Why rangers died (T8)
50-size wilderness scouts attrit 2.4%/turn (50->3/100t). Big mouths starve
fast; intel arrives dead.

## Design (fan-1)
- 4x 5-size scouts at t100 (20 mouths total), 4 diagonal directions, 300km.
  5 mouths forage-trickle (farmland-sustainable; wilderness-lottery).
- Below MIN_ARMY (no missions claim them); HOLD on arrival (watch, don't die
  attacking); reports ride mail automatically (150km/turn).
- Fan (not 1): survival lottery (1-2 of 4 report). 20 mouths for +940.
- Packs invade on scouted stagnants (eater locks); march blind-precise
  (coords known via mail; no live tracking needed vs stagnants).
- Falsifiable: eater+fan vs 4 randoms. Contact? Captures? 1000+?
