# Colony-blitz (demolition, not grind)

## Thesis
- v3's mothers muster (T6: raids donate). But colonies (<300) defend ALONE:
  1 train/town/turn each, max ~30-60 packs, no mutual support in time.
- 3 simultaneous colony-hits split colony-defense the way multiaxis splits
  muster. Mothers can't cover colonies (distance + own muster-tax).
- Each colony-conversion ~= +150-300. Three ~= +500-900. Demolition scores
  (900+) while victims crater (400s). Non-mirror wins => mu breaks 66.
- Falsifiable H2H (quiet box!): blitz vs 2x v3fix-close. Expect 700+ vs
  sub-500s. Then FFA-rate for ordinal.

## Design (blitz-1)
- Locks: foe towns pop 100-300 (colonies! not mothers, not pioneers),
  up to 3, different towns (same-town double-hit wastes).
- Need: garrison + 30 (colonies don't muster-track; flat margin).
- Packs: rich-500 assembly (purpose-tagged), march at need, no hold
  (opportunistic; peace first).
- Abort: colony grows >350 (graduated to mother-class; T6 applies).
