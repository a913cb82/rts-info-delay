# Weakling-eater (specialist demolition)

## Thesis
- FFA-peace vs PEERS is solved (726-tie, all mechanisms). No mu there.
- mu lives in DIVERSITY: vs weaklings (randoms/stubs: stagnant, unmustered),
  captures pay +300 each with near-zero risk. v6 scores 726 vs 4 randoms
  (ZERO captures!) -- it never even tries. A specialist that full-invades
  stagnants while peace-tying peers breaks mu open (726-floor + feasts).
- Falsifiable: eater vs 4 randoms (FFA-ring) should score 1000+ (vs 726).
  Then peers-league (must still tie 725+; no peace-tax).

## Design (eater-1)
- Detect STAGNANT: foe with <=2 towns at turn >= 2000 (never expanded;
  unmustered, unmusterable -- 2 towns = 2 trains/turn vs our 8+).
- Full-invade: locks on ALL stagnant towns (mothers too! no T6 vs weak),
  packs from rich-500s, march at need. Prize-gate low (50+).
- Peers (3+ towns): existing blitz rules (colonies only, T6-abort).
- Risk: misclassify slow-peer as weak (donation!); gate turn>=2000 and
  town-count<=2 strictly. Stagnants never recover (proven: 608-flat).
