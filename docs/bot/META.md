# Meta-bot (composition-modes)

## Thesis
- Every bot plays its own position (lattice/cadence). Nobody reads the FIELD.
- Field archetypes are visible: raiders (foe armies marching at you),
  growers (foe towns multiplying), stagnants (foe towns frozen at 1-2).
- Optimal response differs per composition (RPS-ish):
  - Raiders inbound: full-muster + turtle (deny snowball; S1-pyrrhic them).
  - All-growers: compound-race (cadence-max, presend, zero raid-tax).
  - Stagnants present: eater-invade (free captures, no muster).
  - Mixed: weight by threat (raiders > stagnants > growers).
- v6 has all actuators (muster/raid/vampire/presend) but no selector.
  Meta-1 adds the composition reader + mode switch (hysteresis 500t).
- Falsifiable (quiet!): meta vs v6 in MIXED leagues (predators+randoms+
  growers -- diversity rewards reading; mirror-leagues stay tied by design).
