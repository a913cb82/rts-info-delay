# Boost-timing (T2 growth-window)

## Thesis
- Colonies grow fastest at age ~300-1200t (T2: 750t-optimal, 47->210).
  Boosts outside the window waste (too early: mother-drain for a colony
  that would grow anyway; too late: missed compounding window).
- v6 boosts any colony <150 pop regardless of age (newborns + ancients).
- Age-aware: boost iff 200 <= age <= 1500 (in-window); newborns grow solo
  first (they're fast anyway); ancients are what they are (feeders).
- Falsifiable: +1-5 vs v6 in league (micro-edge stacking for margin).
