# Teacher-city (technique-frontier exploit)

## Mechanics (economy.py _market_improvement)
- Farm-output improvement up to +75% (max_improvement) from services/head.
- Technique frontier: best methods in TEACHING range; range scales with
  teacher pop (fame_range_km: 10km <=1k, ~60km at 5k, 150km max).
- 600-towns teach 10km (useless). 5000-town teaches 60km (+75% all farms!).
- Services propagate one hop/turn (network needs connectivity!).

## Design (teacher-1)
- Phase 1 (t0-3000): v3-spread (feeders to ~500 each, lattice).
- Phase 2 (t3000+): concentrate (stop feeding feeders; boost ONE capital
  via directed boosts (BUILD into it!) to 2000+ (range ~30km), then 5000+.
- Feeders drain uphill (migration) into teacher (sacrifice for range!).
- Phase 3: teacher's +75% lifts the 60km farm-ring (fewer mouths, more each).
- Falsifiable (quiet box!): teacher vs v3. Net = boost - sacrifice - tax.
- Risk: T5-parasite (-60%!) says sacrifice loses; teacher must EARN its
  range via +75% before feeders die. Tight window; may fail. Bold.
