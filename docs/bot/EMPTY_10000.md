musters a pack (capital 2566→615, colonies drained ~1900 each).

aggressive's capital down 640 turns later (t5345). Aggressive's four

Scoreboard of the war: pro 107k (won the war), aggressive 98k/2t
(traded its whole position for one 320-pop town), greedy 129k WON THE

## Per-bot vs GTO/personality

**pro (GTO ref) — war-winner, game-loser.** Post-beheading play PASS
(headless compounding, revenge completed). FAILS: (1) no stay-behind
— drained capital to 320, backstabbed (deterrence violation: never
empty home vs a live third party); (2) forward-settle provocation
unpriced (double-found in greedy's face without cover → colony died,
war started on greedy's terms); (3) revenge pricing open — 640-turn
hunt for aggressive's capital: +EV or compounding loss? (107k <
129k; counterfactual filed). Plan: stay-behind floor (capital keeps
N+1 vs known force, always); provocation check (no border foundings
without pack cover); revenge must price (hunt iff +EV vs compound).

**greedy (impatient) — won by shape.** 3 towns, 12 trains (minimal
spend), shrugged off capital loss, compounded to 129k. Its impatience
≈ optimal in an uncontested game — winning ≠ GTO (no wars to price;
margin-500 untested). DON'T "fix" the winner's formula; add cheap
capital stay-behind (one guard — the t4688 beheading was free for
pro; a single defender may have deterred). Plan: stay-behind one;
otherwise guard the formula (no new spending).

**aggressive (fighter) — brilliant backstab, fatal trade.** Pack
muster PASS, backstab timing PASS (free capital!). FAILS: (1) no
retaliation pricing — stole 320 pop, lost capital + 4 colonies +
war (98k/2t; worst trade of the game); (2) colonies undefended (4
deaths, free kills — tripwires must cost the enemy something or be
abandoned); (3) under-expansion (2 towns; free slots left on table
all game). Plan: price revenge before raiding (backstab iff
stored-force × retaliation-discount still +EV); colony guards-or-
abandon (no free kills); take free slots (expansion floor 3+ towns).

**expander (sprawler) — thin and suicidal.** 14 towns @ ~1.4k avg
(vs greedy 43k/town): sequential NET+ foundings, collective −EV
(tragedy of the commons — site NET is myopic, ignores own future
foundings crowding it). Veto kept it alive (survived!) but poor.
WORSE: 7 single armies fed one-by-one into turtle's 7-stack
(t5282–t9965, 4700 turns, 0 turtle casualties) — stale-intel suicide
loop (never re-verified defenders; never stood down). Settlers path
through known piles. Plan: (1) density cap (no founding within ~65km
of own towns — GTO spacing, kills the tragedy); (2) suicide-breaker
(re-verify defenders with fresh intel before EACH dispatch; 2 failed
raids on same target → stand down 500 turns); (3) settler routing
(around known piles, not through).

**turtle — defended nothing at maximum cost.** 20 trains (steady
1–4/kturn paranoia drip), 7 standing guards, 871 pop (SHRANK from
1347; ~10k pop spent vs settlers!). 7 free kills (expander suicides)
that cost 10k to "earn". Threat model never stood down: treats all
foe armies as raiders (settlers walk TOWARD void, raiders toward ME
— velocity test missing); foe_print discount didn't fire (expander
printed constantly, but printed *settlers*). Plan: (1) velocity test
(inbound-vector toward own towns = threat; else traffic); (2) guard
budget ∝ town pop (7 guards for 871 pop is insane — cap standing
force at ~2× single-town print need); (3) stand-down rule (no raid-
vector foe for 500 turns → release half the guard to compound).

## Validated GTO addenda (proposed §12 entries)

1. Stay-behind: never drain home below N+1 vs live third parties
   (pro t4696 — the backstab tax).
2. Retaliation pricing: raids price the victim's revenge (aggressive
   t4696: +320 pop, −(capital + 4 colonies + war)).
3. Headless wins: beheading is priced, not fatal (greedy won
   beheaded t4688→t10000; pro compounded beheaded t4696→).
4. Suicide-breaker: re-verify defenders fresh per dispatch; 2 fails
   → stand down (expander t5282–t9965).
5. Density cap: own-town spacing floor ~65km (expander 14×1.4k).
6. Settler/traffic distinction: inbound-vector test before mustering
   (turtle 10k vs settlers).
7. Mutual-annihilation watch: 1v1 meetings (t3681–t4616, scouts
   colliding) — route around contact when recon is the mission.
