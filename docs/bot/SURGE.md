  Early mouths are worth ~5% more than late ones (exponential edge).
- Bank is fullest t1-50 (1000ms reservoir). Late turns starve (yield-lock).
  Spend EARLY (overtrain pioneers t1-100, recover after), not evenly.
- v6 trains JIT (even cadence). Surge front-loads: double pioneer-trains
  t1-100 (bank allows), then normal. Same lifetime mouths, younger curve.
- Falsifiable (quiet!): surge vs v6 (early towns +1-2? deadline +3-5?).
